食影筆記隱私權政策
RecipeLens Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日 Effective date: November 14, 2026

本政策說明小墨魚異想天空工作室開發的 Windows 應用程式「食影筆記」（RecipeLens）如何處理你的資料。This policy explains how RecipeLens, a Windows app by 3mofstudy (小墨魚異想天空工作室), handles your data.

摘要

食影筆記在你的電腦上執行。小墨魚異想天空工作室沒有帳號系統，也沒有收集使用者資料的伺服器。你的食譜、設定與 Google 授權都存在你自己的電腦；上傳的檔案直接存到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。

Summary

RecipeLens runs on your computer. 3mofstudy operates no account system and no server that collects user data. Your recipes, settings and Google authorization stay on your own computer, and uploaded files go directly to your own Google Drive.

存在你電腦上的資料

  • 設定（例如輸出位置、上傳格式、選定的雲端資料夾）。
  • Google 授權權杖，存在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\RecipeLens\config\，讓你不必每次重新登入。
  • 產生的食譜、截圖與上傳紀錄，預設存在「文件\RecipeLens」。

這些資料不會傳送給小墨魚異想天空工作室。

Data stored on your computer

  • Settings, such as the output folder, upload formats and the selected Drive folder.
  • Your Google authorization token, stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RecipeLens\config\ so you don't have to sign in every time.
  • Generated recipes, screenshots and upload records, stored in Documents\RecipeLens by default.

None of this data is sent to 3mofstudy.

Google 使用者資料

只有在你選擇上傳到 Google 雲端硬碟時，食影筆記才會請你登入 Google，並只要求 drive.file 權限：查看、編輯、建立及刪除這個 App 所建立或你用這個 App 開啟的檔案。食影筆記無法存取你雲端硬碟中的其他檔案。

使用這項權限做的事：

  • 在「我的雲端硬碟」建立「食影筆記」資料夾，以及你在 App 內新增的子資料夾。
  • 列出這些資料夾的名稱與修改時間，讓你選擇存放位置。
  • 上傳你選擇的食譜檔案（Markdown、PDF、Word）與步驟截圖。
  • 讀取 App 上傳檔案的名稱、ID、連結與是否在垃圾桶，用來避免重複上傳、在上傳中斷後安全重試。

Google 使用者資料只用於上述功能，在你的電腦與 Google 之間直接傳輸，不會傳送給小墨魚異想天空工作室或任何第三方（包括 AI 服務），不會用於廣告，不會出售，也不會由任何人閱讀。

食影筆記使用及傳輸從 Google API 取得的資訊時，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」（Limited Use）規定。

Google user data

Only when you choose to upload to Google Drive does RecipeLens ask you to sign in with Google, and it requests only the drive.file scope: see, edit, create and delete the files that the app creates or that you open with it. RecipeLens cannot access any other files in your Drive.

What the app does with this permission:

  • Creates a folder named 食影筆記 (RecipeLens) in My Drive, plus any subfolders you add in the app.
  • Lists the names and modified times of those folders so you can choose where to save.
  • Uploads the recipe files you select (Markdown, PDF, Word) and step screenshots.
  • Reads the name, ID, link and trash status of files it uploaded, to prevent duplicates and to retry safely after an interrupted upload.

Google user data is used only for these features and travels directly between your computer and Google. It is not sent to 3mofstudy or any third party (including AI services), not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by any person.

RecipeLens's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

影片與 AI 服務

食影筆記從 YouTube 或 Facebook 下載你貼上網址的公開影片、字幕與資訊欄。整理食譜時，會把字幕、資訊欄文字與影片截圖交給你電腦上安裝的 ChatGPT（Codex，由 OpenAI 提供）或 Claude（Claude Code，由 Anthropic 提供），使用你自己的帳號處理，適用該服務的隱私權政策。語音辨識在你的電腦上執行。

Google 雲端硬碟中的任何資料都不會傳給 AI 服務。

Videos and AI services

RecipeLens downloads the public video, subtitles and description from the YouTube or Facebook link you paste. To write the recipe, it passes the subtitles, description text and video screenshots to ChatGPT (Codex, by OpenAI) or Claude (Claude Code, by Anthropic) installed on your computer. They are processed under your own account and that service's privacy policy. Speech recognition runs on your computer.

No data from your Google Drive is ever sent to an AI service.

錯誤回報

轉換失敗時，食影筆記會詢問是否把錯誤摘要回報給開發者；只有你同意（或事先開啟自動修復與回報）才會送出。報告包含去除憑證後的錯誤訊息、處理階段、所用 AI 平台、App 版本、作業系統版本與報告編號，不包含影片、字幕、食譜、設定檔、Google 使用者資料或任何憑證。報告存放在開發者的 Google 試算表，只用於修正問題。

Error reports

When a conversion fails, RecipeLens asks whether to send an error summary to the developer. It is sent only if you agree, or if you turned on automatic repair and reporting beforehand. A report contains the error message with credentials removed, the processing stage, the AI platform used, the app and OS versions, and a report ID. It never includes videos, subtitles, recipes, settings files, Google user data or credentials. Reports are kept in the developer's Google Sheet and used only to fix problems.

刪除資料與撤銷授權

  • 撤銷 Google 授權：到 Google 帳戶的「第三方連結」 移除「食影筆記」，或刪除電腦上的 config\google_token.json。
  • 本機資料：刪除輸出資料夾與 %LOCALAPPDATA%\RecipeLens，或從 Windows「設定」解除安裝。
  • 雲端檔案屬於你，可以在 Google 雲端硬碟直接刪除；撤銷授權不會刪除它們。
  • 錯誤報告：來信附上報告編號，我們會在 30 天內刪除。

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Revoke Google access: remove RecipeLens from third-party connections in your Google Account, or delete config\google_token.json on your computer.
  • Local data: delete the output folder and %LOCALAPPDATA%\RecipeLens, or uninstall the app from Windows Settings.
  • Files in your Drive belong to you and can be deleted in Google Drive. Revoking access does not delete them.
  • Error reports: email us with the report ID and we will delete it within 30 days.

安全

與 Google、AI 服務及錯誤回報接收端的連線都使用 HTTPS。Google 授權權杖只存在你 Windows 使用者帳號的資料夾中。

Security

Connections to Google, AI services and the error-report endpoint use HTTPS. Your Google token is stored only in your Windows user profile folder.

兒童

食影筆記不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會刻意收集兒童的資料。

Children

RecipeLens is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

政策變更

政策更新時，會修改本頁並更新生效日期。涉及 Google 使用者資料用途的重大變更，會在 App 內另行告知。

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes to how Google user data is used will also be announced in the app.
